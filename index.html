FAQ

Answers to help you feel confident.

Do I need to connect my bank accounts?

No. ReceiptWise is designed to work without bank connections so you stay in control.

Can I keep everything on-device?

Yes, by default receipts are processed on-device and you decide what to sync.

How accurate is receipt scanning?

Our OCR focuses on totals, merchants, and categories, with easy manual edits.

Is the AI coach required?

Not at all. The AI coach is optional and can be enabled anytime.

Will you support teams or families?

Yes. Shared folders are built for households, freelancers, and small teams.
